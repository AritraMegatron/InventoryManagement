IMPORTANT
These are fictional observations created for a software demonstration. The address is real, but the unit, measurements, commercial facts, people and observations are not verified and are not issued by DLF.

SITE SUMMARY
- Proposed inline unit is approximately 780 sq. ft. and sits near a fashion-to-food transition area.
- Frontage appears adequate for a beverage and dessert counter, but seating capacity inside the unit may be limited.
- Broker quoted monthly base rent of INR 4.85 lakh. Separate draft terms mention INR 5.10 lakh and a 7% revenue-share clause. This needs reconciliation.
- Broker indicated 6 months' security deposit; draft terms require 8 months.
- Broker handover estimate is 15 September 2026; draft commercial terms state 1 October 2026.

DEMAND OBSERVATIONS
- Tuesday lunch traffic was steady but not crowded. Office workers and young adults were visible between 13:00 and 14:00.
- Saturday evening traffic was substantially higher, with family groups and teenagers moving toward food and entertainment zones.
- Cold beverages, ice cream and handheld desserts appeared well suited to the observed traffic pattern.
- Demand is likely to be strongest on Friday evenings, weekends, holidays, school vacations and hot-weather days.
- No audited footfall report was provided. Broker's weekly footfall estimate should be treated as unverified.

COMPETITION
- Several national coffee, ice-cream, bakery and quick-service brands operate within the broader mall.
- A generic shake-and-sundae concept may face direct competition. Strong visual presentation, rapid service and a differentiated signature product will be necessary.
- Premium pricing may be possible, but value bundles and smaller trial sizes should be tested.

ACCESS AND LOGISTICS
- The mall is in the Sector 18 commercial district and is served by major roads and public transport. Exact walking time from metro exits was not measured.
- Customer parking is available at the mall, but weekend vehicle congestion could slow pickup and staff arrival.
- Service-corridor access looked narrow during the visit. Large deliveries should be scheduled in the morning window.
- Draft terms restrict regular delivery access to 07:00-09:30; broker brief says 07:00-10:00.
- No dedicated exterior signage was observed for the hypothetical unit location.

TECHNICAL RISKS
- Mechanical exhaust approval is unresolved. This may limit the menu to beverages, desserts and low-heat preparation.
- Power information is inconsistent: broker mentions up to 60 kW, while draft terms assume 45 kW pending approval.
- Confirm drainage, water inlet, grease management, fire-load approval and waste movement route before signing.
- Confirm whether delivery-platform riders have a designated pickup location and whether pickup queues can block customer flow.

RECOMMENDED FOLLOW-UP
1. Obtain audited weekday/weekend footfall by daypart for at least six months.
2. Reconcile rent, CAM, deposit, revenue share, handover date and fit-out period in one signed term sheet.
3. Obtain measured plan, final power letter, exhaust feasibility and food-service approval.
4. Model occupancy cost at conservative, base and upside sales scenarios.
5. Compare the site with two existing outlets of similar format and rent-to-revenue ratio.
6. Test a compact beverage-led menu that avoids open flame and minimizes back-of-house complexity.
